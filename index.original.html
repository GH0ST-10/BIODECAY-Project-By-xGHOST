// The outbreak

Civilization is gone. The air isn't safe either.

BIODECAY drops you into a world that ended without you. Cities are hollow, the dead walk the streets, and every breath outside is a gamble.

Built from the ground up by xGHOST as one cohesive experience, not a pile of mods. Scavenge what's left, fortify what you can, and survive long enough to learn what happened.

// Field manual

Everything out here wants you dead.

Six things to know before you step outside.

01

The infected

The dead don't rest. They roam the ruins in numbers, and the nights belong to them.

02

Scavenge the ruins

Pick through abandoned buildings for food, tools and supplies. Every run is a risk worth weighing.

03

Fortify and hold

Turn a wreck into a safehouse. Reinforce it, stock it, and make it a place worth coming back to.

04

A hostile world

Zombies aren't the only threat. Contamination, hunger and injury will finish what they start.

05

Arm yourself

Find it, fix it, or build it. What you carry decides whether you fight or run.

06

Total immersion

Sound, lighting and atmosphere tuned so the world feels as dead and dangerous as it looks.

// Deployment

Three steps to the wasteland.

  1. Download the pack

    Hit the download button below. The file starts downloading straight away.

  3. Launch and survive

    Start the instance, create a world, and try to make it through your first night.
